01

The Problem

As a BCA student, my life is a constant juggle of assignments, projects, exams, and personal goals. There's always more to do than hours in the day.

Assignments

Stacking up with overlapping deadlines

Projects

Multiple coding projects running simultaneously

Exams

Preparing while managing everything else

Personal Goals

Side projects and learning goals getting pushed aside

Existing productivity apps remind you about deadlines. They send notifications, show countdowns, and track dates. But they don't help you actually complete the work. That's the gap I wanted to solve.

02

Why I Built It

I wanted to create something that doesn't just organize my tasks — it actively helps me finish them.

Most to-do apps are passive. You add a task, set a date, and hope you remember to do it. But the real problem isn't remembering — it's figuring out what to work on right now, how to break it down, and how to stay focused long enough to actually get it done.

DuePilot AI was my answer to that. An AI-powered platform that acts less like a calendar and more like a coach — helping you plan, prioritize, and push through your work.

03

How It Works

User Input
Tasks / Goals
AI Processing
Personalized Plan
User Action
Progress / Analytics

The user provides their tasks and goals. AI processes them, considers deadlines and patterns, and generates a personalized plan. The user acts on it, and the system tracks progress to improve future recommendations.
